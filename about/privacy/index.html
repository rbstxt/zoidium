Privacy Policy

Reference summary (this is not the Privacy Policy text)

Zoidium requires no account, is free to use, has no advertising, and does not use advertising trackers. It has no mechanism to send or store projects or editing data on Zoidium's servers. The public Web version uses Cloudflare Web Analytics (shown as Cloudflare Insights in some browser network panels) to measure aggregated site usage and performance. The Electron version runs locally.

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) explains how information is handled in Zoidium, provided by 1zworks (the “Operator”).

1. Panzoid's official Privacy Policy

If you use Panzoid's services, website, account, or features managed by Panzoid, please separately read Panzoid's official Privacy Policy. Panzoid's handling of information is outside this Policy. For its Terms, see Panzoid's official Terms.

2. Information Zoidium intentionally does not collect

Zoidium does not provide account or transmission features that collect the following information:

  • Account or login information, name, address, or telephone number
  • The contents of projects, videos, images, audio, materials, or rendered files being edited
  • Advertising identifiers, behavior profiles, or information used for advertising tracking

Zoidium displays no advertising and does not upload Your Work to the Operator. If you upload or share something yourself with another service, that service's policy applies.

3. Information stored on your device

In the browser version, settings and plugin enablement may be stored in local storage such as localStorage. Project files are saved to and loaded from locations you choose. This information is not sent to the Operator's servers; you manage it through your browser settings and file operations.

Storage, caching, and permission behavior provided by your browser or operating system are governed by their respective providers' specifications. In the Electron version, the application and its embedded server run on your device.

To start CM3, the Web and development Electron modes fetch the configured CM3 source page and its same-origin runtime resources into the Git-ignored `.zoidium-resources/` cache when it is absent or refreshed. `npm run build:web` and `npm run dist` reuse or create the same cache during their build. This bootstrap can expose the usual request metadata, such as IP address and user agent, to the source host, but it does not send project contents or editing data. The cache remains on the development machine after the local server ends; only disposable build trees are removed, and a static deployment contains the generated files required by that deployment.

4. Cloudflare on the Web version

The public Web version at zoidium.1zworks.com uses Cloudflare for Web delivery and security. Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare Insights) is also enabled to understand site performance and usage. It is used to aggregate page-loading and similar performance information, not for advertising or individual behavior tracking. Zoidium does not send project contents or account information to this analytics feature.

Information processed by Cloudflare, including retention, is governed by Cloudflare's service settings and policies. See Cloudflare Web Analytics and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy for details.

When you connect to the website, the delivery platform or hosting provider may handle technical information such as your IP address, browser information, referrer, access time, and requested URL as needed for delivery, troubleshooting, and protection against unauthorized access. This does not mean that Zoidium collects the contents of Your Work.

5. Contact messages

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address needed to reply and the information included in your message. We handle it only for responding, confirmation, and record-keeping as long as necessary, and do not use it for advertising.

For questions about information handling, confirmation, or deletion requests, contact hello@1zworks.com. If Zoidium holds no information about you, we will say so.

6. External links

This page links to third-party pages such as Panzoid and Cloudflare. Information handling on those pages is governed by each provider's policy. Opening a link takes you from Zoidium to that service.

7. Changes to this Policy

The Operator may update this Policy when the service changes or to respond to legal requirements. Updated content takes effect when posted on this page. For important changes, we will make the update clear where reasonably possible.
