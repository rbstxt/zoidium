---
title: Create meshes with Geometry+
sidebar:
  label: Geometry+
---
Add a procedural mesh to a 3D Scene.

1. Open the scene's **Objects** picker.
2. Choose a shape under **GEOMETRY+**.
3. Edit the shape's numeric properties.
4. Assign a material.

Choose Rounded Box, Polyhedron, Cone, Capsule, Tube, Gear, Helix, or Spline.
For a hollow cylinder, choose Tube and adjust its wall thickness. For a swept
path, choose Spline and edit its points. Set its interpolation and closed-loop
option to match the path.

[Material+](/docs/features/material-plus/) provides PBR and node-based materials.

Control reference: [geometry-plus](/docs/reference/geometry-plus/).
